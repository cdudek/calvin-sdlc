# Audiences

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-27. Personas: [personas.md](personas.md). Jobs: [jobs-to-be-done.md](jobs-to-be-done.md).

## Primary: designed, priced and marketed for first

1. **Product teams building with coding agents.** 3 to 30 people at startups and scale-ups: product manager, UX designer and product engineers, where agents write much of the UI. Personas: Priya (PM), Dana (designer), Ben (engineer). Usually bought by the engineering lead.
2. **Founders and solo builders.** Entrepreneurs and hobby project developers steering agents themselves; they start free and bring a team later. Personas: Eva (founder), Hugo (hobby developer).

Why these two: the multiplayer core is the product team's daily problem, and solo builders are the way in. Both share the job of steering agents with exact feedback; teams add deciding together.

## Secondary: supported, not designed around

- **Agencies and freelancers** building for clients with AI: clients as guests, sign-offs on record, several organisations per person. Personas: Alex (agency lead), Chris (client).
- **QA and design QA specialists** checking builds against specs and designs.
- **Engineer-led agentic teams** where product engineers also own UX, with no dedicated designer or PM (owner's addition). They use the same flows with fewer roles; reading of the owner's note by Claude, to confirm.

## What this means for decisions

- When a choice serves the primary and hurts a secondary audience, the primary wins.
- Agency-only features (client-branded portals, client widgets, invoicing of change requests) wait until the primary audiences are served.
- Marketing speaks to product teams and solo builders; agencies get a page, not the homepage.
