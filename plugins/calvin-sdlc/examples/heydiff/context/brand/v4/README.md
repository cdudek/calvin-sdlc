# HeyDiff logo files, v4

Version 4, 30 Oct 2026. These are the master files of the HeyDiff identity. The brand guide (HeyDiff Brand Guide canvas, page 35) points here.

The logo: two speech bubbles, each with one square corner, coral on the left, blue on the right, violet where they overlap, next to the lower-case wordmark `heydiff`. Lockup 5:4 (mark 5 units tall, wordmark 4), gap 1 unit.

## Rules

- Place these files. Never rebuild the lockup from the mark and typed text.
- Lockup at least 24 px tall. Under 24 px, use the mark alone, flat, from 16 px.
- Clear space: the height of the e on every side. App icons and avatars are the exception: the mark sits at 60 percent and the icon edge is its clear space.
- The wordmark is always one colour.
- Mac menu bar: the knockout mark as a template image, 16 pt (guide page 30).

## Retired

Do not use earlier exports: `brand/v3` (mark F, round bubbles with curved tails), the 03A pack (`heydiff-03A`) and the logo v1 and v2 export folders.
