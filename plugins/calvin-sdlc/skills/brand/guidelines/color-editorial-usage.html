Colour system for editorial and ad assets

Applies to presentations, whitepapers, reports, social and event assets. Not to dashboards and website/app mockups; there the UI colours apply.

Title slides and chapter dividers: decoration element in the bleed

Place a decoration element in the bleed of the slide. Make sure it fits in colour-wise. Released are 03, 04, 06, 07, 08, 11, 14, 16, 17 (random selection). Rotation: 07, 08, 11, 17 freely rotatable, 03, 04, 06, 14, 16 only in 45° steps. All other elements are not permitted on title slides and chapter dividers; for other asset types there is no restriction. Depending on the space on the slide up to 3 elements of the same kind. Place them at different angles (rotation) in the bleed of the slides to ensure OMR recognition. With a tweak to remove.

Clarification of sections 6 and 8. This rule applies and takes precedence over the sentence "no decoration on title slides". What is meant there: no free-standing decoration in the content area and no pattern. Decoration elements sit exclusively in the bleed, behind the content, and never over text. Patterns stay excluded on title slides and chapter dividers. Decoration and pattern are never mixed.

Choice without tweak

If the brief says nothing, the asset type decides:

  • Presentation, slides, event and social assets → A
  • Whitepaper, report, PDF, document → B

If the asset type is unclear, ask, do not guess.

Chapter divider

Always the same variant as the title slide of the same document, background as well as bar. No switching within a document.

Forbidden on title slides and chapter dividers

Light tones as background: #ada6fc, #8e7bff, #33cccc, #80e0db, #f5c41c, #ffdb75. Not even with their darker counterpart as the bar. This pairing looks weak and is not permitted for titles.

Likewise: #ffffff as background, #f2b200 and #00adad as bar colour.

Decoration elements

Permitted when they sit at the edge or in a corner, never behind or over the headline and never behind the bar, never in the foreground. Released are only 03, 04, 06, 07, 08, 11, 14, 16, 17; 07, 08, 11, 17 freely rotatable, 03, 04, 06, 14, 16 in 45° steps only.

Decoration elements are two-coloured. Both colours are chosen freely from the nine brand-kit colours :

  • Purple #8259ff strong · #8e7bff medium · #ada6fc soft
  • Mint #00adad strong · #33cccc medium · #80e0db soft
  • Yellow #f2b200 strong · #f5c41c medium · #ffdb75 soft

Every combination is allowed, within a family (Purple Strong + Purple Soft) as well as across families (Yellow Strong + Mint Soft). There is no fixed mapping from background to decoration colour. Contrast between the two decoration colours is allowed and welcome, but not required; quiet combinations are as permitted as strong ones.

Binding: only these nine colours, no #000000 and no #ffffff, neither as fill nor as outline. Neither decoration colour may equal the background colour , otherwise the affected part becomes invisible; check before setting. The two decoration colours are never identical, at most two colours per element. No background patterns on title slides and chapter dividers.

Colour tweak — chooses the combination

The colour tweak does not set both colours to the same value. It chooses the combination. The element has two colour slots: A (primary) and B (secondary). On clicking a swatch:

  1. Slot A takes the clicked colour.
  2. Slot B keeps its previous colour family. The step within the family may be adjusted if the combination holds up better that way.
  3. Slot B changes family only if A and B would otherwise be identical or if B equals the background colour.
  4. A and B differ after every tweak. Never both slots the same.
  5. Neither A nor B equals the background colour after the tweak.

Example: before Mint Strong + Yellow Soft, click on Purple → Purple Strong + Yellow Strong. Wrong would be Purple Strong + Purple Strong or everything in purple.

Check — title slide and chapter divider

  1. Background is #000000 or #8259ff, no light tone.
  2. Variant A or B fully implemented, not mixed.
  3. Chosen variant named and justified (asset type or tweak).
  4. Chapter divider identical to the title slide.
  5. Decoration at the edge, not behind headline or bar, at most two colours.
  6. Decoration only from the release list (03, 04, 06, 07, 08, 11, 14, 16, 17), rotation as specified.
  7. Both decoration colours come from the nine brand-kit colours, no black, no white. Name both hex values.
  8. No decoration colour equals the background colour. Name the background hex as well.
  9. The two decoration colours differ.
  10. After a colour tweak: name both colours before and after and confirm that not both were set to the same value.
  11. No pattern.

Most reliable choice

On coloured backgrounds the black bar is the most reliable choice. Coloured bars belong on black or white.

One bar colour

One single bar colour per visual. Geometry, padding and line height come from the tokens in tokens/highlight.css.

4 · Text colour

Rule

Text is #000000 or #ffffff, by contrast to the background. No exceptions in body copy, headlines, sublines, captions.

Coloured text — three cases only

  • Numbers and labels that refer to a chart series and take its colour
  • Large key figures when the number itself is the design element
  • Small status flags and markers for orientation

Forbidden pairings

In all three cases WCAG AA applies. No #000000 on #8259ff, #8e7bff or #00adad. Kein #ffffff on #f2b200, #f5c41c, #ffdb75, #ada6fc, #33cccc or #80e0db.

5 · Variation budget

Per slide

  • One surface colour
  • One accent colour
  • Neutral as much as needed
  • Data colours only where there is data

Together therefore at most two colours beyond black and white, and the second only when there is data.

Across a document

What may vary: single slides on a light tone, bg-primary-subtlest, bg-secondary-subtlest, bg-surface-elevation-2 instead of white, one accent slide in Purple Strong between white content slides, one data slide with a different data colour. Two consecutive slides do not have the same layout in another colour.

What does not vary: title slide, chapter dividers, bar logic, text colour. Those are the recognisable constants.

6 · Attitude

Do

Data-driven, clear, professional. Calm surfaces, plenty of white space, few shapes. Colour is used to show something, not to decorate something. When in doubt, one colour less.

Never

More than two chromatic colours on one surface, gradients, drop shadows, patterns behind text, free-standing decoration in the content area of title slides, coloured body copy.

7 · Tweaks

These specifications are defaults, not locks. If the brief explicitly sets something else, the brief applies.

  • titelfarbe: black (variant A) | purple (variant B) | white-bar
  • white-bar: background #000000, bar #ffffff, bar text #000000 — only on explicit instruction. The loudest appearance, for cases where A feels too quiet.
  • kapiteltrenner: wie-titel | own colour
  • akzentfarbe: purple | mint | yellow
  • balkenfarbe: neutral | farbig
  • decoration: an | aus (bleed decoration on title and chapter dividers)

Without a specification the defaults from sections 2 and 3 apply. There is never a silent deviation. A deviation is named in the result.

8 · Self-check, per slide

  1. One surface colour, one accent colour, at most two chromatic colours?
  2. Text exclusively #000000 or #ffffff, except in the three permitted cases?
  3. Bar colour matches the background, one bar colour in the visual?
  4. Title slide and chapter dividers in the same variant (A or B), no light tone, without pattern, decoration only in the bleed at the edge?
  5. No forbidden contrast pairing?
  6. Data colours only in charts and key figures?
