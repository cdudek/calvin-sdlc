Bullets

Bullet lists carry a small square in Purple Strong #8259ff. Square side 0.42 em, no rounding, indent 1.04 em. The marker sits on the cap centre of the first line and stays there for multi-line entries. Class .omr-list.

Numbering — default: bare digit

The default is the bare digit: two digits with a leading zero, Purple Strong, OMR One Extrabold, tabular figures, without surface. This is also the default form of chapter numbers in the table of contents. Class .omr-list-numbered.

Large numbering — Störer

The Numbered Super-Nova is the shape for large numbering: three steps above boxes placed side by side, chapter openers, single large numbers. Side length 2.2 em, digit in text size. Free-standing as .omr-number-nova, as a list via .omr-list-numbered.nova. In small numbered lists it is not set.

1

Bedarf klären. Die Zahl steht über der Box, nicht in der Zeile.

2

Anbieter vergleichen. Drei Boxen nebeneinander, drei Zahlen.

3

Entscheiden. On black the shape turns white, the digit black.

Not permitted

No round dots, dashes, ticks or icons as markers. No second colour family in the same list, Mint and Yellow are not bullet colours. No purple marker on a purple background. No Störer in small numbered lists — there the bare digit stands; no Störer shape other than super-nova for numbers, no digit that leaves the safe inner area. Markers and numbers do not count against the colour budget but replace no accent colour: a purple square justifies no second purple accent on the same surface.
