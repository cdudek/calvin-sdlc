OMR logo database

34 lockups: the umbrella brand OMR, 12 brands and 21 products. Each exists horizontally and vertically, in black and white. The filled box marks a Brand, the outlined one a product or service under a brand. This box logic is the brand hierarchy; it is never swapped. Exceptions without a vertical version: OMR single logo, OMR X.

Background, logo and H1 together

The colour version of the logo and the colour of the H1 follow the same rule. Examples in colour world 2, brand kit.

Never permitted: a black logo or black H1 on Purple Strong, Purple Medium or Mint Strong.

Decision tree

1  Which OMR brand or product is the asset for?
   → if the brief does not say, ask, always in this wording (verbatim, in German):

     Welches Logo soll verwendet werden
     1. OMR
     2. OMR Reviews
     3. OMR Education
     4. Andere: z. B. OMR Festival, Podcast, 5050 etc.

   → on 4, ask for the exact lockup and look it up here
   → without an answer no logo is set

2  Is it about OMR as a whole, the cosmos or the Festival?
   → OMR single logo (slug "omr")
   → lockup "OMR Festival" only when explicitly requested

3  A specific brand or product?
   → exactly one lockup from the database above. One logo per asset.

4  Orientation: horizontal, unless the format does not allow it → vertical

5  Colour version: by background, maximum contrast

Special cases and pitfalls

Umbrella brand OMR

The single logo stands for OMR as a whole, for the entire cosmos and for the Festival. It is the default when nothing more specific applies. The OMR Festival lockup only on explicit instruction. Monochrome, no box, no vertical version.

OMR X

Brand tier, horizontal only. At roughly 4:1 the box is much narrower than the other 6:1 to 13:1; in very narrow layouts it is the only lockup that still works horizontally. A vertical lockup is a request to the brand team, not something to rebuild yourself.

OMR Y removed

The brand no longer exists. The lockup was deleted from the database without replacement on 15.09.2026: no slug y, no files, no replacement. If you come across it in an old asset, it is not replaced but removed.

OMR Manager and OMR Viewer

Two SaaS products of OMR Reviews, in the inventory since 15.09.2026: Manager for profile management, Viewer for buyer-intent data. Product tier, outlined box, cluster OMR Reviews. Horizontal and vertical, black and white. When it is about the product, its lockup stands alone, not next to the Reviews logo.

OMR Podcast

Used to be a brand of its own, today a product of the umbrella brand OMR. The outlined box is correct. In architecture diagrams and brand overviews the podcast belongs on the product tier.

Podstars by OMR

Its own design system. Never serve it from this inventory.

File names of the originals

vertikal instead of vertical for Podcast and Purple Stage, an ampersand in the Jobs & HR path, OMR X without an orientation suffix. In this database all names are normalised to <slug>_<black|white>_<h|v>.svg , so those pitfalls no longer apply.

Two geometry generations

One set with lockup height 27.46 and box radius 2.47, one set with 30.76 and 2.77. Proportionally identical, pure export size, no difference in meaning. When mixing them in one layout, scale to the same optical height, not to the same viewBox number.

Inner spacing

The distance between the OMR wordmark and the box is constantly around 13 % of the lockup height and is part of the file. It is never changed, rebuilt or replaced by placing mark and text side by side yourself.

Do & Don't

Do

  • Before placing, clarify which brand or product the asset is for
  • Horizontal as standard, vertical only when the format forces it
  • Colour version by background, maximum contrast
  • One logo per asset
  • Scale the original file proportionally

Don't

  • No logo on suspicion when the brand is unclear
  • Do not swap brand and product boxes
  • No rebuilding from the OMR mark plus typed text
  • No colours of your own, no gradients, no effects, no drop shadow, no outline
  • Do not rotate, mirror, distort, crop or mask
  • No second logo directly beside it

Open points

  • Clear space, the minimum distance to other elements, is not encoded in the SVGs
  • Minimum sizes for print and digital are missing
  • Behaviour on photos, whether darkening is prescribed
  • Co-branding rules next to partner logos
  • Release of the internal brands Crew, A-Team, Logistics, Travel, Run Club for external communication
