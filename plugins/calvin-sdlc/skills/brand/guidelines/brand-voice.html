Do “Your event starts on 6 May at 9:00 — tickets from 50 €.”
Don’t “Your Event Starts On 06.05.2026 - Tickets ab 50€.”
  • Address the reader directly — German du (lowercase), English you.
  • Sentence case everywhere. No trailing period on buttons/labels.
  • En dash for ranges (6–8 May); narrow NBSP before units & € in German.
